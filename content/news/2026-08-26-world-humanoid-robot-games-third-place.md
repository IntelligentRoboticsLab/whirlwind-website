We finished 3rd in the 5v5 Middle Division at the World Humanoid Robot Games 2026 in Beijing, held together with the RoboCup Asia-Pacific (RCAP) Beijing Masters.

The Games ran from 22 to 26 August in the National Speed Skating Oval, the "Ice Ribbon" built for the 2022 Winter Olympics. Across the whole event, 666 teams entered 51 competitions, from football and athletics to household and rescue tasks. Our tournament was a knockout of 16 teams, playing two halves of 15 minutes on a 22 by 14 metre pitch.

We beat SC ROB-X and Yuxin Zhanqing to reach the semi-final, where HTWK Robots were too strong for us. We then beat HyperYe 3:1 in the match for third place. B-Human won the tournament, beating HTWK Robots 13:3 in the final.

A year ago, at the first edition of the Games, we went out in the quarter finals. This time we came back with the robots and software from RoboCup 2026 in Incheon, plus a month of work since, and finished on the podium.

Thanks to everyone on the team for the long days in Beijing, to the other teams for the games, and to the organisers of the World Humanoid Robot Games and RoboCup Asia-Pacific for the tournament.
