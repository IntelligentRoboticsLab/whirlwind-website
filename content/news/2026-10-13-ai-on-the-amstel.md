On Tuesday 13 October, we were invited to participate in the RobotCon event hosted by AI on the Amstel. We played a showmatch during the main event in front of a full stand in Amsterdam's biggest basketball arena, Sporthallen Zuid. A joint team of our goalie and "RoboCop", the quadruped from the Dutch police faced two of our robots in an exciting 2 versus 2 match.

We also participated in the dance-off, where our robots showed off their moves to the audience. Our robot gave it their best shot, but was just barely edged out by the winning Unitree G1 which had the loosest hips!

Before and after the main event, we also had people try and stop some shots from the robots which was a lot of fun.

Thanks to everyone who came to watch, and to the organisers of AI on the Amstel for having us.
