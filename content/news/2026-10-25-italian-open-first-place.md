We won the Middle Division at the 2026 RoboCup Italian Open in Rome.

This was the first edition of the Italian Open, an official RoboCup regional event for humanoid soccer. It ran from 23 to 25 October at the Gazometro Ostiense as part of [Maker Faire Rome](https://makerfairerome.eu/), with teams from Italy, Germany, the Netherlands and China. The pitch was set up in the middle of the fair, so most of our games were played with a crowd along the boards.

It is also our first tournament win. This year we finished 3rd at the German Open, 4th at [RoboCup 2026 in Incheon](/news/2026-07-02-robocup-fourth-place) and 3rd at the [World Humanoid Robot Games in Beijing](/news/2026-08-26-world-humanoid-robot-games-third-place). This time we're coming home with the trophy.

Thanks to everyone on the team, to the other teams for the games, and to the organisers of the Italian Open and Maker Faire Rome for the tournament.
