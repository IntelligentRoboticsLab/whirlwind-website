whIRLwind has won the Middle Division at the 2026 RoboCup Italian Open in Rome!

This was the first edition of the Italian Open, an official RoboCup regional event for humanoid soccer. It ran from 23 to 25 October at the Gazometro Ostiense as part of [Maker Faire Rome](https://makerfairerome.eu/), with teams from China, Germany, Ireland, Italy, the Netherlands, and Poland. The pitch was set up in the middle of the fair, so most of our games were played with a large crowd.

Over the course of the tournament we saw the efforts from the entire year come together, culminating in a great 5-0 final match against the HULKs where we also committed zero fouls for the first time. Combined with reworked strategy and our newly developed dribbling among other improvements, we were able to take the first place trophy home to the Netherlands for the first time in the Humanoid Soccer League history!

The Italian Open was our final competition of 2026. In this year we also finished 3rd at the [German Open](/news/2026-03-14-german-open-third-place), 4th at [RoboCup 2026 in Incheon](/news/2026-07-02-robocup-fourth-place) and 3rd again at the [World Humanoid Robot Games in Beijing](/news/2026-08-26-world-humanoid-robot-games-third-place). We are looking forward to the 2027 season, where we will continue to improve our gameplay and strategy.

Finally, we would like to thank everyone on the team for their efforts, the other teams for the matches we played, our sponsors for their continued support, the organisers of the Italian Open and Maker Faire Rome for the tournament, and you the reader of this article for following our journey. Let's keep pushing the boundaries of RoboCup together!
