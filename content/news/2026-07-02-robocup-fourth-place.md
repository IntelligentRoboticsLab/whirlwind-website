We finished 4th in the Humanoid Soccer League at RoboCup 2026 in Incheon, South Korea.

RoboCup is the largest robotics and AI competition in the world, and this was the first time in its history that it was held in South Korea. From 30 June to 6 July, Songdo Convensia filled up with more than 3,000 competitors, researchers and students from dozens of countries, plus around 15,000 visitors coming to watch.

This year was also the first edition of the Humanoid Soccer League, where we play in the Middle Division. We came in with the same robots we took to the German Open in March, where we finished 3rd after having had them for only two weeks. That left us about four months to improve the framework into one that could hold up over a larger scale tournament. 

Ending up 4th in that field, in the league's first year, is a result we're very happy with. It also gave us a very concrete list of things to work on, and we've already started on most of them in preparation for the World Humanoid Robot Games 2026 in Beijing next month.

Thanks to everyone on the team for the long days in Incheon, to the other teams for the games, and to the organisers in Incheon for putting on the tournament.
